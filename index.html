Export in 1 minute

  1. 1
    Open Telegram Desktop

    Export doesn't exist on mobile. Get it at desktop.telegram.org.

  2. 2
    One chat: open it → ⋮ → Export chat history

    All chats (best for Pulse): Settings → Advanced → Export Telegram data, tick personal and group chats.

  3. 3
    Format: “Machine-readable JSON”

    Untick photos and videos so it's fast. Then drop result.json here.

🔒 Everything runs in your browser. Only small numbers from each check-in (no message text) are kept in this browser so the next Pulse can compare.
